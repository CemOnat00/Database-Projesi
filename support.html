Frequently Asked

How do you authenticate a work?+

Each piece arrives with a certificate of authenticity signed by the curator and the artist's studio where applicable.

Can I cancel or reschedule a workshop?+

Yes — anytime up to 48 hours before the session, from your dashboard. Full refunds are issued automatically.

Do you ship internationally?+

Worldwide. All shipments are crated by hand, insured, and tracked door-to-door.

What is the return policy?+

14 days, insured both ways. Reach out and we will arrange collection.

How do discount codes work?+

Codes (e.g. CURATED10) can be applied at checkout or on the workshop reservation page. One code per order.
